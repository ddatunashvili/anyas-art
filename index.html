The Studio

Characters built from the armour inward

Missiah is a digital painter focused on character portraiture: grimdark sci-fi, gothic fantasy and original characters brought to life for players, writers and collectors.

Every commission starts with the person behind the character — their history, their scars, their faith. From full reference sheets to moody single portraits, each piece is painted to feel lived in.
